---
title: Suppressions
description: Prevent email delivery to recipients that should no longer receive messages.
---

Suppressions are email addresses that Dugble should not send to. They protect sender reputation and help prevent repeated delivery attempts to recipients who bounced, complained, or were manually suppressed.

## Suppression origins

Every suppression records an `origin`:

- `bounce` — created because of a delivery bounce.
- `complaint` — created after a complaint signal.
- `manual` — added directly by your team.

A suppression can also contain a `source_id` when it was created from another event or resource.

## Add a manual suppression

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.dugble.com/suppressions \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $DUGBLE_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "email": "ada@example.com"
  }'
```

Email addresses are normalized to lowercase. Attempting to add an address that is already suppressed returns a conflict response.

## Retrieve by ID or email

The suppression identifier can be either the suppression UUID or the email address:

```http
GET /suppressions/{suppression}
DELETE /suppressions/{suppression}
```

This makes it easy to check or remove a suppression when your application only has the recipient email address.

## Bulk operations

Dugble supports adding or removing up to 100 suppressions in one request.
