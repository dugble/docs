title: Contacts

Contacts are recipient profiles that your team can reuse across audience and messaging workflows. A contact can store an email address, phone number, name, SMS consent state, custom properties, topic subscriptions, and segment memberships.

## Contact data

A contact can include:

- `email` — required and unique within the team.
- `phone` — optional international phone number.
- `first_name` and `last_name` — optional profile fields.
- `sms_consent_status` — `unknown`, `opted_in`, or `opted_out`.
- `sms_consent_source` — `api`, `import`, or `manual` when explicit SMS consent is recorded.
- `unsubscribed` — email unsubscribe state.
- `properties` — application-specific contact data.

Dugble normalizes valid phone numbers to E.164 and records the detected country.

<Warning>
A contact with the same email address or phone number cannot be created twice within the same team.
</Warning>

## Create a contact

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.dugble.com/contacts \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $DUGBLE_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "email": "ada@example.com",
    "phone": "+233201234567",
    "first_name": "Ada",
    "last_name": "Lovelace",
    "sms_consent_status": "opted_in",
    "sms_consent_source": "api",
    "properties": {
      "plan": "pro",
      "company": "Analytical Engines"
    }
  }'
```

## Build audiences with contacts

Contacts become reusable audiences through two complementary features:

- **Topics** represent subscription preferences, such as product updates or newsletters.
- **Segments** group contacts for targeting and bulk messaging workflows.
