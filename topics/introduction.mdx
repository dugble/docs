---
title: Topics
description: Model subscription preferences that contacts can opt into or out of.
---

Topics let you represent the kinds of messages a contact can subscribe to, such as product updates, newsletters, or account announcements.

Each topic has a name, optional description, default subscription behavior, and visibility.

## Default subscription

Choose one of two defaults when creating a topic:

- `opt_in` — contacts are subscribed unless they explicitly opt out.
- `opt_out` — contacts are not subscribed unless they explicitly opt in.

The default is part of the topic's subscription model and cannot be changed through the topic update operation after creation.

## Visibility

Topics can be:

- `public` — suitable for preferences that may be shown to contacts.
- `private` — intended for internal audience logic.

New topics default to `private` when visibility is omitted.

## Create a topic

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://api.dugble.com/topics \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer $DUGBLE_API_KEY" \
  --header "Content-Type: application/json" \
  --data '{
    "name": "Product updates",
    "description": "Major product announcements and release notes",
    "default_subscription": "opt_out",
    "visibility": "public"
  }'
```

## Contact subscriptions

A contact's effective state for a topic is exposed as either `opt_in` or `opt_out`. You can retrieve and update those states through the contact-topic endpoints.
